## 📱 Android apps

Free. No accounts. Installed from an APK, so your phone will warn about an "unknown source" — that's normal for a direct download.

| App | What it does | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| 🎓 **Scholartunities** | Scholarship search that matches funding to your nationality, study level and subjects — official sources first, honest warnings when a listing needs a second look. Your profile never leaves the phone. Ages 13+. | [Download](https://scholartunaties.netlify.app/) |
| 📈 **Fundmark** | Personal finance tracker that runs entirely on your phone. Bank balance and physical cash tracked separately, savings goals, budgets, Excel export. PKR and USD. Nothing leaves the device. | [Download](https://fund-mark.netlify.app/) |
| 📷 **Huekai** | A camera that colour-grades real photos so they feel like they belong in an anime world — Cyberpunk, One Piece, AOT, Ghibli, Solo Leveling, Death Note. Not a cartoon filter; skin stays natural. *Early test build.* | [Download](https://github.com/Ahmed-Hassan-811/Huekai) |

## 💻 Web apps

Nothing to install. Open and use.

| App | What it does | Open |
|---|---|---|
| 🔐 **CodeVault** | 169 copy-ready snippets across 9 languages — searchable, syntax-highlighted and runnable in the page. Every snippet is bundled in the file, so it works with the internet completely off. | [Open](https://codevault811.netlify.app/) |
| 🌱 **Harila** | Plant and crop questions answered in plain language — what's wrong, what to do, and exactly how much to feed. Two growing modes, from a pot on a balcony to an open field. | [Open](https://harila.netlify.app/) |
| 📋 **Nexus Project Forge** | Describe what you want to build and get back the functions it actually needs, the order to build them in, why each step sits where it does, and a sprint plan that fits your deadline. | [Open](https://nexusprojectforge.netlify.app/) |
| 🤖 **Synthetic Focus Group** | Usability testing without waiting on a reviewer from every field. Upload an interface, pick a persona, set a goal — get friction analysis, detected issues and UX suggestions back. *Built with Gemini 3.* | [Open](https://the-synthetic-focus-group-298442072248.asia-southeast1.run.app) |
| 🎤 **VoxCoach** | Speaking practice for languages, interviews, presentations and meetings. Record your turn and the coach replies out loud, in a persona you choose, with feedback in English or Urdu. | [Open](https://vox-coach.streamlit.app/) |
| 🧬 **TAMG** | Target-Aware Molecule Generator — LLM-proposed molecular candidates with substructure constraints, every one validated by RDKit for validity, QED, SA score and Lipinski. | [Open](https://ahmed-hassan-811-tamg.streamlit.app/) |
